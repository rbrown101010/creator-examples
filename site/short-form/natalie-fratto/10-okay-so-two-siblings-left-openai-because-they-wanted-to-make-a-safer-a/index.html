Anthropic started as an OpenAI breakup in 2020 when a brother, VP of Research Dario Amodei, and his sister Daniela, a VP of Safety and Policy, left OpenAI together.

OpenAI uses a process called RLHF, where the model's answers pass through a panel of real humans who rate them to decide which is better before providing an output.

Anthropic pioneered constitutional AI, where humans wrote an overarching constitution for the model to guide its behavior, and then the model checks itself against that constitution.

Is this truthful?

Is this violent?

The resulting output is less likely to go off the rails, and enterprise legal and compliance teams often trust more.
