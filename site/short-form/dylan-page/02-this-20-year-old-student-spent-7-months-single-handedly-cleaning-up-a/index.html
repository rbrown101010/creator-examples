This 20-year-old student spent 7 months single-handedly cleaning up a polluted river in India, and the before and after images are so staggering people believe this is AI.

This is Bitu Tabahi.

And back in January, he became tired of waiting for the government to clean up the Ajnora river.

So he decided fine, I'll do it myself.

And let me tell you, bro went through hell.

You see, Bitu had zero financial support, meaning he had to start doing this without any safety gear, cleaning up trash with his bare hands, which unsurprisingly caused multiple infections on his skin.

But on top of that, Bitu had to dodge snakes.

He had to be careful not to fall in the river because he couldn't even swim.

And to make it even worse, instead of locals seeing this and helping him, many actually made fun of him and told him to come clean up their drains instead.

But, despite all of that, Bitu, he just kept on pushing, slowly transforming this riverbed, piece by piece.

And at one point, he was even moving 10 kg of trash every single day, and eventually, as the months went by, people started to take notice.

And today, families and visitors are returning to this riverbed for the first time in years.

The world needs more people like Bitu.
